What is the use of memoization?

What is Memoization?
Memoization caches the results of expensive computations so repeated work is avoided.
Why Memoization in Query Optimization?
Problem: Exponential explosion of equivalent plans
A query can have many equivalent plans. Without memoization, the optimizer may:
Recompute the same subexpression multiple times
Recalculate costs for identical subtrees
Miss opportunities to share common work
